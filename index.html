My Journey with IQ Option

Honest and straight to the point — no fake success stories.

I first heard about IQ Option from YouTube around 2022. At the time, there was a flood of content claiming "easy profits", "millions per month", and so on. I was curious, but I decided to try the demo account first for a few weeks.

The demo account is excellent — $10,000 virtual balance, responsive platform, full access to all features with no capital required. This is where I learned to read charts, experiment with indicators, and understand market rhythms. It's a genuine plus I can't deny.

After 3 weeks on the demo and starting to find consistency, I made my first $50 deposit. The deposit process was smooth — local bank transfer was available and funds arrived within hours. I started with digital options at small sizes, $1–$2 per trade.

Honestly? The first two weeks I lost money. Not because the platform cheated, but because I was too impulsive and had no proper money management. An expensive lesson that could have been avoided if I'd been more patient with the demo.

After that I studied more deeply — using DXY as a reference, support/resistance levels, and disciplined TP/SL. From there I started finding consistency. Bottom line: the platform is a tool. Results depend on your strategy and discipline.

Tips Before You Start

Things I wish I knew from the beginning.

01 //

Take the Demo Seriously

At least 2–4 weeks on the demo until your win rate is consistent. Don't skip this step.

02 //

Only Risk What You Can Lose

Only deposit money you're truly willing to lose. Never use funds for living expenses or borrowed money.

03 //

Money Management

Limit each trade to a maximum of 2–5% of your total balance. One bad oversized trade can wipe out your capital.

04 //

Verify Your Account Early

Upload your ID documents from the start. Don't wait until withdrawal — the process takes time.

05 //

Don't Trust Signal Sellers

Telegram groups selling paid trading signals are almost always scams. Learn to analyze on your own.

06 //

Understand the Risk
